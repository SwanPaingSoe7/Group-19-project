Beginner’s Skincare Guide

Simple tips to help you start your skincare journey.

1. Read Ingredients

Check the ingredient list to understand what the product contains and what it does.

2. Patch Testing

Test a small amount on a small area of skin before using a new product widely.

3. Basic Routine

Start with the basics: cleanser, moisturiser, and sunscreen during the daytime.

4. Introduce Slowly

Add new products gradually and observe how your skin responds.
